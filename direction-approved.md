  1. *Artistic Poetics & Atmospheric Depth:* Inspired by *Alto's Adventure*, *Monument Valley*, and *Sky: Children of the Light*. Tranquil, elegant, deeply immersive cosmos.
  2. *3-Layer Parallax & Ethereal Interactivity:* Touch inputs produce gentle luminous ripples (aura rings), constellations breathe with rhythmic photon pulses, and pointer movement creates subtle optical parallax.

  4. *Astral Glass UI & Tactile Softness:* High-legibility typography, frosted astral glass dialogs, gentle specular light sweeps, and rewarding celestial snap feedback during puzzle solving.
