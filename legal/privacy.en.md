**Last updated:** 2 November 2026 · **Version:** 3.0

Thank you for playing Nozvi. This policy explains what data we collect when you play Nozvi on Android or on the web (together, the **"Game"**), why we need it, who receives it, and how you control it.

**At a glance**

- The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number.
- When you choose Agree at the start, the Game turns on gameplay statistics, error reports and personalised ads. You can turn each off at any time and still play fully.
- The Android version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are personalised only if you agree; if you decline you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
- You can change your choices at any time in **Settings, under Privacy**.
- We do not sell your data.

## 2. What information do we collect?

The data we process comes from three sources: information you give us, information collected automatically when you play, and information from partners. Automatic collection depends on your platform and your privacy choices.

### 2.1. Information you give us

The Game does not require registration. We only receive information you choose to send, for example when you:

- email us with a question or a privacy request: your email address, your message, and Your ID if you include it;
- choose settings in the Game, including your privacy choices (stored on your device).

### 2.2. Information collected automatically

**Data stored on your device (all platforms).** Level progress, achievements, sound, motion and language settings, your privacy choices, and a random Statistics ID. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us, except the Statistics ID when you agree to statistics.

**Gameplay statistics.** Through GameAnalytics:

- *Identifiers:* the random Statistics ID created by the Game; session ID and session number.
- *Gameplay:* levels started, completed or abandoned; achievements; moves and restarts; session length.
- *Device:* manufacturer and model, operating system and system browser versions, connection status, Game version.
- *Location:* country, which GameAnalytics derives from your IP address.

**Error diagnostics.** Through Sentry:

- *Sessions:* one record each time the Game starts, used to measure the share of sessions without errors.
- *Error reports:* the error message and where it occurred in the code; the level, chapter and mode being played; the steps just before the error (screen changes, levels completed or abandoned, hint ads watched, Game warnings).
- *Device:* device type, operating system, browser, page address, Game version.

Error reports are not linked to the Statistics ID or any user ID.

**Advertising data.** When ads are shown, Google AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and your interactions with ads, according to your choices in section 4.

**Web access data (web).** When you open the web version, our hosting provider GitHub Pages records your IP address and browser information in its access logs.

### 2.3. Information from partners

We receive one piece of technical information from Google: when you finish a rewarded ad, Google tells the Game on your device so it can grant your hint. This confirmation is not sent to our servers.

We do not receive ad profiles, browsing history or per-player ad revenue, and we do not buy personal data about you from third parties. We only see AdMob's aggregated reports (such as total impressions and revenue), which are not linked to any individual.

### 2.4. What we do not collect

Your name, email (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, GPS location, photos, microphone audio or payment data. The Game has no in-app purchases and no chat.

## 4. Your privacy choices

You control whether non-essential data is shared. Turning these items off never affects your ability to play.

**Consent before processing.** When you first start playing, the Game states clearly that choosing Agree also turns on gameplay statistics, error reports and personalised ads, and that you can turn each off in Settings. Until you choose Agree, these services are not initialised and receive no data. Only the services present in the version you use are listed.

**Changing your choices.** You can review and change each choice at any time in the Game's privacy settings. Changes take effect immediately:

- **Statistics:** when you withdraw consent, we stop sending, delete unsent events on your device and issue a new Statistics ID so that later data is not linked to earlier data. Before the ID changes, the Game gives you the chance to keep your current ID for use in a request to delete data already sent.
- **Error reports:** when you withdraw consent, the error reporting service stops immediately and sends no further data.
- **Personalised ads:** when you withdraw consent, ads loaded afterwards are not personalised.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal and does not automatically delete data already sent; see section 10 to request deletion.

**Advertising.** Some versions of the Game show ads provided by Google AdMob, including rewarded ads that you watch only when you choose to.

- **When Personalised ads is on,** ads may be personalised based on your device's advertising ID and your past activity.
- **If you turn Personalised ads off,** you still see ads, but they are not personalised; they are chosen by context and approximate location.
- **Even when ads are not personalised,** Google still processes your IP address and device information, and may use the advertising ID for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

In some regions Google may show an additional consent notice required by local law.

**Device settings.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your operating system settings (on Android: Settings, Google, Ads).

## 6. Local storage and similar technologies

The Game does not use cookies. It uses the browser's or app's local storage (localStorage) to keep:

| Content | Purpose | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Progress, achievements, settings, language | Run the Game | Always |
| Privacy choices | Remember your choices | Always |
| Random Statistics ID | Group statistics events from the same player without knowing who you are | Created on first launch; leaves the device only if you agree to statistics |
| Unsent statistics events (up to 2,000 records) | Send later when online | Only with statistics consent; deleted when you switch statistics off |

On Android, Google AdMob may read your device's advertising ID as described in section 4. You can delete all local data by uninstalling the Game, clearing app data, or (on the web) clearing site data.

## 7. How is your data protected when transferred internationally?

The providers in section 5 may process data outside the country where you live, specifically: error reports are stored in the United States (Sentry); statistics are processed by GameAnalytics, a company in Denmark (European Union); advertising and web access data are processed by Google and GitHub in the United States. For such transfers we rely on appropriate safeguards applied by these providers, such as:

- contractual commitments by the provider to a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA, as Singapore's transfer limitation obligation requires;
- the security standards and certifications the provider publishes;
- a cross-border transfer impact assessment dossier, prepared and filed with the personal data protection agency under Article 20 of Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP.

Contact us to learn more about these safeguards.

## 8. How do we protect your data and how long do we keep it?

**Security.** Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Only the Nozvi team can access the statistics and error dashboards. No system is completely secure, but we apply reasonable measures to protect your data.

**Retention.** We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described.

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Data stored on your device | Until you uninstall the Game or clear app data |
| Gameplay statistics | Up to 12 months on a rolling basis, then GameAnalytics deletes or anonymises it (only aggregate figures remain) |
| Error diagnostics | 30 days, then deleted automatically |
| Your emails and privacy request records | 12 months after the request is resolved |
| Advertising data | Per Google's policy |
| Web access logs | GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and keeps them under its own policy; we cannot access these logs |

## 9. Children's privacy

The Game's content is suitable for all ages, but **the Game is not directed at children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age at which they can consent under the law where they live.

**For parents and guardians.** If you believe your child agreed to share data, switch the options off in **Settings, under Privacy** and contact us with Your ID. We will delete the related data where possible.

## 10. What are your rights and how can you exercise them?

### 10.1. Your rights

Depending on the law where you live, you have the following rights over your personal data:

- **Right to be informed** about the processing of your personal data, as in this policy.
- **Right to consent, refuse and withdraw consent:** at any time in Settings, under Privacy.
- **Right to view, rectify or request rectification** of your personal data.
- **Right to request provision and deletion** of your personal data and **restriction of processing**.
- **Right to object** to processing.
- **Right to stop receiving advertising** based on your personal data; in the Game, switch off Personalised ads in Settings or contact us.
- **Right to complain, denounce, sue and claim compensation** for damage in accordance with law.
- **Right to request protective measures** for your personal data.

These rights follow Articles 4 and 28 of Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15. Players in Singapore have corresponding rights under the PDPA, including access, correction and withdrawal of consent.

**Where to complain:** in Vietnam, the personal data protection agency of the Ministry of Public Security (Department of Cybersecurity and High-Tech Crime Prevention, A05); in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

### 10.2. How to exercise them

| You want to | How |
| --- | --- |
| Stop sharing | Settings, under Privacy: switch each option off. Effective immediately. |
| Delete data on your device | Uninstall the Game or clear app data. |
| Access or delete statistics already sent | Copy **Your ID** from Settings, under Privacy, and send it to support@alpaca-solutions.com. We forward the request to GameAnalytics (which processes the data on our behalf) and let you know the result. The scope and timing of deletion depend on the provider's capabilities; in any case statistics data is deleted or anonymised after at most 12 months. Aggregated figures no longer linked to Your ID cannot be separated out for deletion. |
| Error diagnostics | Error reports are not linked to any user ID, so we cannot identify yours. They are deleted automatically at the end of the period in section 8. |
| Advertising data | Switch off Personalised ads in Settings, reset the advertising ID on your device, or use Google's tools at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com). |

Note: the new Statistics ID created when you switch statistics off only unlinks future data; it does not delete data already sent.

### 10.3. How we handle requests

We respond within 2 working days of receiving a valid request and complete it within the periods set by Article 5 of Decree 356/2025/ND-CP:

| Request | Period | If a processor or third party must act |
| --- | --- | --- |
| View, correct or provide data | 10 days | 15 days |
| Withdraw consent, restrict or object to processing | 15 days | 20 days |
| Delete data | 20 days | 30 days |

For complex cases we may extend once and will tell you why. We ask for more information only when truly needed to verify a request, and may refuse requests that are unlawful, with reasons. Exercising your rights does not affect your gameplay.

## 12. Definitions

- **Personal data:** information relating to a specific person or that helps identify a specific person, including online identifiers.
- **Statistics ID (Your ID):** a random string created by the Game on your device, containing no personal information, used to group statistics events from the same player.
- **Advertising ID:** an identifier Google assigns to an Android device, which you can reset or delete.
- **Controller:** the party that decides the purposes and means of processing.
- **Service provider (processor):** a party that processes data on behalf of, and under instructions from, the controller.
- **EEA:** the European Economic Area: the EU member states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.
